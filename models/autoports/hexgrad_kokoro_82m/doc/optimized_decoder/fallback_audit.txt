Optimized decoder runtime fallback audit (tt/optimized_decoder.py)
==================================================================
Command: grep -nE "to_torch|from_torch|\btorch\." tt/optimized_decoder.py

Findings:
- to_torch: 0 occurrences anywhere in the module (callers slice at the torch
  boundary in tests via ttnn.to_torch, outside the measured path).
- from_torch / torch.*: confined to
    * from_state_dict()  (setup-time host->device weight conversion, incl. the
      packed-QKV torch.cat), and
    * prepare_inputs()   (host-side input id/mask construction).
- The forward path methods _embed, _albert_layer (packed QKV linear,
  nlp_create_qkv_heads, scaled_dot_product_attention, nlp_concat_heads, dense,
  layer_norm, fused-gelu FF1, ffn_output), _encode, prefill_forward,
  capture_decode_trace, and decode_forward contain NO torch / from_torch /
  to_torch calls.
- The measured traced-decode window is ttnn.execute_trace + ttnn.synchronize_device
  only (see tests/test_perf_optimized.py::test_perf_decode).
- No tilize/untilize/reshard/host-fallback ops in the measured path beyond the
  fused nlp_create_qkv_heads / nlp_concat_heads TM ops that are the intended
  optimized head path, and the two cheap ttnn.reshape adaptors around them (no
  layout conversion; add/remove a leading unit dim).

Conclusion: clean. No unnecessary torch/from_torch/to_torch, tilize/untilize,
reshard, or host fallback in measured prefill/decode.
