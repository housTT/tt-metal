 something strange: the shadow of the tree seemed to move faster than the sun itself.

Elena was puzzled. She ran to the village elder, Mr. Thorne, who was known for his wisdom and love of riddles. “Mr. Thorne,” she asked, “why does the shadow move so quickly?”

Mr. Thorne smiled and said, “Ah, Elena, shadows are like time itself—they move faster when the light changes quickly. But tell me, what do you think causes the sun to rise?”

Elena thought for a moment. “I think it’s because the earth turns, right?”

“Exactly!” Mr. Thorne exclaimed. “And now, let’s solve a riddle together. If the earth rotates once every 24 hours, how many times does it rotate in a week?”

Elena counted on her fingers. “Seven days, so seven times?”

“Not quite,” Mr. Thorne chuckled. “Think about it—how many hours are in a day?”

“Twenty-four,” Elena replied.

“Right! So, how many hours are in a week?”

“Seventy-two,” Elena said confidently.
