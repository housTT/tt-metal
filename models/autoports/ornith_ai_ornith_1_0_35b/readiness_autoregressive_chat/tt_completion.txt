
Here's a thinking process:

1.  **Analyze User Input:**
   - **Topic:** How a rainbow forms
   - **Audience:** Curious ten-year-old
   - **Requirements:** Short, clear explanation

2.  **Identify Key Scientific Concepts:**
   - Sunlight (white light) contains all colors
   - Raindrops act like tiny prisms/glass spheres
   - Light refracts (bends) when entering the raindrop
   - Light reflects off the inside back surface of the raindrop
   - Light refracts again when exiting the raindrop
