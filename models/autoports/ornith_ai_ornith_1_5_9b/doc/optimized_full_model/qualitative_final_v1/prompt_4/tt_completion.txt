The user wants me to translate "Hello, how are you today?" into French.

Let me think about this translation.

"Hello" can be translated as "Bonjour" (formal/greeting) or "Salut" (informal).

"How are you today?" - this is asking about someone's current state/condition.

The most common translation would be:
- "Bonjour, comment allez-vous aujourd'hui ?" (formal)
- "Bonjour, comment vas-tu aujourd'hui ?" (informal)

The word "today" (aujourd'hui) is a bit unusual in this context.
