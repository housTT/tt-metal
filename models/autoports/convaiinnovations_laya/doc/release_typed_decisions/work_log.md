- 00:40: design decision for the bucket set. The server (`server/engine.py`) keeps one flat row-bucket list for every seq bucket and writes `X-Laya-Batch` from its own chunk plan, and `server/` may not be edited by this track. The engine therefore gets per-seq row lists (`LAYA_ROW_BUCKETS_<seq>`, here `LAYA_ROW_BUCKETS_1024=1,2,4,5,8,10,16`), reports the union as `row_buckets` so the server's limits and the 128/256/512 chunk plans stay bit-identical to the English bundle, and chunks any call above the largest row bucket of its seq bucket into replays of that bucket (`tt/engine.py: _run_device`). For the served package the manifest sets `LAYA_MAX_BATCH_TOKENS=16384` so the server's own plan never exceeds 16 rows at 1024 and the `X-Laya-Batch` header stays exact; the engine chunking remains the safety net for `/v1/forward` callers.
