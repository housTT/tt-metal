- `/home/hous/dev/clef/tt-metal/ttnn/cpp/ttnn/operations/transformer/chunk_gated_delta_rule/device/chunk_gdn_device_operation.cpp` lines 41 to 43 and 58 to 59: the device op validates `q`, `k`, `v` as `BFLOAT16` and `g`, `beta` as `FLOAT32` (`check_gdn_tensor`, "chunk_gdn: {} has wrong dtype").
- `/home/hous/dev/clef/tt-metal/ttnn/cpp/ttnn/operations/transformer/chunk_gated_delta_rule/chunk_gated_delta_rule.cpp` lines 39 to 43 and 181 to 190: the host wrapper casts any non-bf16 q, k, v to bf16 before the device op ("GPU-style mixed precision: q/k/v are bf16 (gate/decay and state stay fp32). Cast here so any caller (fp32 or bf16) feeds the kernel bf16 q/k/v") and any non-fp32 g, beta to fp32. The docstring in `chunk_gated_delta_rule_nanobind.cpp` lines 318 to 322 says the same ("cast to bf16", "cast to fp32").
- `/home/hous/dev/clef/tt-metal/ttnn/cpp/ttnn/operations/transformer/chunk_gated_delta_rule/device/chunk_gdn_phased_program_factory.cpp` line 210: the prep kernel's q, k, v circular buffers are `Float16_b`.

Consequence: an fp32 cast of q, k, v after the conv is a bit-exact round trip (bf16 to fp32 to bf16) and cannot change the kernel's inputs; the kernel does not reject fp32 inputs, it casts them back. The only places before the op where fp32 changes the values the kernel receives are the causal FIR conv (4 taps multiplied and accumulated in bf16 today, `models/experimental/gated_attention_gated_deltanet/tt/ttnn_gated_deltanet.py` lines 220 to 228: `ttnn.multiply` then `ttnn.addcmul` per tap, then `ttnn.silu`, every output bf16) and the beta sigmoid (`ttnn.sigmoid(b)` on the bf16 projection output). The flag is therefore implemented as: conv input upcast to fp32, fp32 conv taps, fp32 FIR multiply-accumulate and SiLU, q, k, v sliced in fp32 and handed to the op (which rounds them to bf16 once), beta computed as sigmoid of the fp32 upcast of `b`; the conv carry returned to the bf16 persistent buffer (exact: the carry holds the raw bf16 conv inputs). The orchestrator's fallback ("cast only after the conv") is void under this contract and is not implemented. Casting q, k, v to fp32 before the op, as the amendment names, would only add three typecast ops per layer and chunk with no numerical effect; the implementation leaves them in the fp32 conv output dtype and lets the op cast once.

The substitution evidence this is judged against (`/home/hous/dev/clef/reports/stage1_gdn_substitution_L30_T8192_gatefp32.json`, torch fp32 core on identical inputs): all TT inputs 0.999962 mean, HF q, k, v with the TT gate and beta 0.999994, HF beta alone 0.999960 (no gain), HF g alone 0.999963. The q, k, v gain there comes from HF's bf16 projection and conv values, not from a dtype into the kernel; the fp32 conv recovers the conv part of it, the bfp8 projection part is the `proj_bf16` variant.
